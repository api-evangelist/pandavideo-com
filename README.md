Panda Video provides a comprehensive video hosting platform for businesses and creators, featuring AI-powered tools such as automatic dubbing, subtitles, and content personalization. The service includes robust security measures like watermarking and encryption, as well as a suite of video management features like playlists, analytics, and live streaming. With flexible pricing plans and a focus on ease of use, Panda Video aims to help creators and enterprises deliver engaging video experiences at scale.

**Areas:** Video Hosting · AI · Business · Creators · SaaS

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Panda Video: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
